# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-25

Rental Manager is designed as an offline-first app.

## Data Collection

- The app does not require internet access for normal operation.
- The app does not upload your business records to a remote server by default.
- Data is stored locally on your device (SQLite database).

## Data Stored Locally

- Inventory and stock data
- Customer details
- Rental/order records
- Invoice and payment history records
- Business profile details used in PDF output
- App settings preferences

## Backups

- You can manually export and restore local backups.
- Backup files may contain sensitive business data.
- You are responsible for where backup files are stored and shared.

## Notifications

- Local notifications are used for reminders on your device.
- Notification data is generated from local app records.

## Your Control

- You can edit or delete records directly in the app.
- You can remove backup files from your device storage at any time.
