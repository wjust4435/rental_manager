Rental Manager is an offline-first app to manage your entire rental business without any internet dependency.

Features:
* Manage customers with full contact details and rental history
* Create orders and generate proforma and final invoice PDFs
* Track inventory items, quantities, and availability
* Payment Ledger to view outstanding balances per customer
* Payment History with payment and refund records
* Business profile configuration for invoices
* Local backup and restore support
* Customizable settings: theme, compact cards, date/time format, and notifications

Designed for small businesses that rent any type of items and need simple, reliable, fully offline record keeping.
