Rental Manager is an offline-first app to run daily rental operations without internet dependency.

Key features:
- Customer management with active and history views
- Proforma and final invoice PDF generation
- Payment Ledger for outstanding balances
- Payment History with payment and refund records
- Business profile and tax profile configuration
- Local backup/restore support
- Settings for theme, compact cards, date/time display, and notifications

Designed for small businesses that rent any type of items and need simple, reliable record keeping.
